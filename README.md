## Main project: systematic trading research

A personal research platform for market-data processing, strategy backtesting and machine-learning evaluation.

- **Python & C++:** research automation, data pipelines, native components and local dashboards.
- **ML & XGBoost:** feature preparation, classification, rolling retraining and model selection.

- **Reproducibility:** model and dataset lineage, saved prediction audits and Python/native consistency checks.
